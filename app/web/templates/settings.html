Integrations

Watchlistarr needs Seerr to send requests, a free TMDB API key to read metadata (v1.7.0: TMDB is the only metadata source), and — optionally — Radarr, which unlocks disk-space and library-count rules. Keys come from Settings → General in your Seerr/Radarr and from themoviedb.org (free account).

Schedule

How often Watchlistarr re-checks your watchlists for newly added films. Scoring and Letterboxd ratings run automatically as part of each sync, so adding a film to Letterboxd is enough — no buttons to press.

Storage guards

Global rules applied to every watchlist. Leave blank to disable each one.

Skip films already known

How Watchlistarr works

  • Pulls movies from your watchlists on a schedule.
  • Enriches each with genre, country, language, year — no login needed.
  • Scores everything with your point sliders.
  • Sends the top movie(s) to Seerr, respecting quotas and disk rules.
  • Remembers what it already added, so nothing is requested twice.
